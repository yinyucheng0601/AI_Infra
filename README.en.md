## Go beyond reading. Explore the system.

AI Infra is a growing collection of design research and interactive experiences for AI infrastructure. It brings information from architecture diagrams, code, and technical documentation into experiences you can navigate, manipulate, and understand step by step.

- **See the structure.** Move from clusters and cabinets to drawers, components, and packages, connecting the system view to its physical parts.
- **Follow the process.** Trace model computation, compiler transformations, and communication flows to see how objects and their relationships change.
- **Explore the design methods.** Discover Patterns and Skills that connect technical research with visual and interaction design.
